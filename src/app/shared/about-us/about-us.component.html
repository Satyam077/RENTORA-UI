What is Rentmizo?

Rentmizo is a modern Property Management System designed to streamline the complexities of rental management. We help landlords save time, avoid late payments, and manage their entire portfolio digitally with ease and efficiency.

Rent Collection

Automated payment tracking and processing.

Tenant Management

Centralized tenant profiles and communication.

Maintenance Tickets

Efficient tracking and resolution of repairs.

Payment Reminders

Automated alerts to avoid late payments.

Agreement Storage

Secure digital storage for lease agreements.

Property Dashboard

Comprehensive overview of your portfolio.
